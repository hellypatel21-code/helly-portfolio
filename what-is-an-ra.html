In my own words

What is an RA?

An RA, or Resident Assistant, is a student who lives on the floor they look after, which means the job does not really clock out when I close my door. Day to day, I am usually the first person residents come to when something goes wrong, whether that is a maintenance issue at an inconvenient hour, a roommate situation that has been building for weeks, or just being homesick and needing someone to notice. I also plan programs and events for the floor, because a hallway full of strangers does not turn into a community on its own, and something as small as a study night or a floor dinner can change that. Part of the role is upholding university policies, and I have learned that you can do that while still treating someone with respect instead of making them feel like they are in trouble. The part nobody warned me about is how much of this job is simply listening, since most of what people bring to me is less about the problem they are describing and more about wanting someone to hear them before anyone tries to fix it.

For me, being an RA mostly means being a familiar face in a place that can feel very unfamiliar at first. I remember exactly how it felt to move somewhere new and not know a single person, so if I can make that first semester a little less lonely for someone on my floor, I would call that the job done well.

What that looks like

Being around

Duty nights, rounds, and an open door the rest of the time.

Floor programs

Study nights, socials, and events that give people a reason to meet each other.

Sorting things out

Roommate conversations, maintenance requests, and figuring out who to call.

Keeping things safe

Following up on policies and knowing when to bring in professional staff.

Pointing you places

Campus resources, good food, and where that one building actually is.
